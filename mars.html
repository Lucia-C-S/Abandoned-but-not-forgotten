Mars: Robots in the Dust

Mars lies farther away beneath a thin, dusty sky. No human has walked there; every mark in this archive was made by a machine. Some explorers lasted months. Others outlived their plans by years. Two are still at work.

WHEN THINGS GO WRONG

Not every mission
finds its way home

In 1999, two Mars spacecraft were lost. Mars Climate Orbiter disappeared because two teams used different units: one metric, one not. Mars Polar Lander fell silent while trying to land. Both losses taught engineers to check every number twice, and helped make later journeys safer.
