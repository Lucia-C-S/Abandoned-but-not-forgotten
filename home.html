STATUS // REFRAMED

When a mission ends, its machines usually stay where they are. A rover cannot turn around and drive home. A lander has no fuel for the climb back into space. And every kilogram brought to Earth asks for more rocket to carry it there.

So "abandoned" does not mean "littered." It means the work is done and the machine has found its last resting place.

But "not forgotten" is just as true. Old rovers taught us to build new ones. Their data still has stories to tell. Some machines still work, and orbiting cameras keep watch over those that have gone quiet.
